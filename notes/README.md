# Development notes

These are design notes from the development repository, shipped **verbatim** because
the paper companion cites them: several of its rows rest on a derivation recorded here.

They are working records, not polished documentation.  Their internal references —
implementation plans and their decisions, the audit record, research probes, other
notes, and the development repository's conventions (module paths under
`implementations/`, `PYTHONPATH=.` from its root) — point into the source repository
and mostly do not resolve in this one.  Each note records how a result was reached as
of its date; where a note is older than the code, the code, tested by `run_tests.py`,
is the authority.
